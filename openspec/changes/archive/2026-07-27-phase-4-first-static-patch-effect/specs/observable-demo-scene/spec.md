## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Exhaustive demo derives and causally proves the complete effect surface
The deterministic demo SHALL freeze expected effect capability, slot, control, parameter, serialized-leaf, rejection, scalar, and audio-observation coverage from the production registries, configs, descriptors, serializers, and focus resolver before dispatch. It SHALL exercise Chorus Amount and Depth through semantic PATCH input and require exact bidirectional expected/exercised equality with no processor-specific duplicate coverage list.

#### Scenario: Exhaustive effect controls execute
- **WHEN** the production fixture's first Patch is traversed in the demo
- **THEN** read-only Chorus identity, Amount, and Depth appear in exact focus/projection order; both scalar controls receive reversible fine/coarse edits; every state/page/text/fixed-snapshot value and EventRecord agrees; and missing or unexpected coverage remains zero

#### Scenario: Versioned effect schema is incomplete
- **WHEN** an effect registry/config/slot/focus/scalar/observation leaf is missing, renamed, duplicated, or appears unexpectedly in StateTree, PatchPageProjection, TextProjection, ParameterSnapshot, or the report
- **THEN** schema-surface equality and demo completion fail even if audio remains nonzero

**Contract facet — causal effect behavior and preservation.**
The deterministic demo SHALL measure the first-Patch effect stage through the production renderer and SHALL prove instrument→effect→mix order, target-only pre/post difference, nonzero stereo side energy, unconfigured-Patch equality, two independently stateful/tailing Chorus instances in a focused graph, and exact effect config/layout preservation across the adjacent preset and both engine replacements.

#### Scenario: Simultaneous Patches are observed
- **WHEN** the configured first Patch and at least one unconfigured Patch render controlled nonzero signals
- **THEN** only the configured stem has a causal pre/post Chorus difference before mixing and all unrelated Patch state, scalar values, and stems remain exact

#### Scenario: Structural sequence completes
- **WHEN** the demo performs adjacent preset, SoundFont-to-Braids, and Braids-to-default-SoundFont replacements
- **THEN** every Ready checkpoint retains the exact Chorus slot/config/layout, produces finite nonzero target output through Chorus, and reports zero fallback and callback destruction

#### Scenario: Effect consequence is fabricated
- **WHEN** a mutant credits changed text/state, final-output drift, synthetic wet input, another Patch, shared native state, or a post-hoc report edit instead of the declared stage measurement
- **THEN** at least one exact effect predicate fails and the demo cannot report completion
