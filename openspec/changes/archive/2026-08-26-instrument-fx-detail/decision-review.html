Lifecycle vocabulary and activation truth

2 choices needed
Current system

OpenSpec asks for

Every Instrument/FX Detail row should distinguish all those phases and keep the old active value beside the requested value until activation finishes.

1A. Which lifecycle vocabulary should Instrument/FX Detail own?

This decides whether Loading, Validating, and Unavailable are real Instrument/FX states or Sample-only states.

1B. What should “active” mean while the graph is activating?

Today the prepared configuration is committed before the audio graph acknowledges activation.

Unblocks OpenSpec tasks 2.6, 3.4, and the lifecycle portion of 6.3.
