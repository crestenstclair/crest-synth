Retained proof targetmake demo-live-semantic-view-model

03
Complete · 31 Jul 2026

Expandable effects and bus topology

Expanded the fixed effect foundation into a bounded, observable routing model with real-time-safe graph exchange.

Retained proof targetmake demo-live-effects-and-buses

Delivered features

  • Up to three ordered, descriptor-driven effect slots per Patch.
  • Explicit buses, sends, returns, and routing identities with at most eight returns.
  • Complete structural graph preparation and exchange outside the audio callback.
  • Semantic, validated, observable, and recoverable topology changes.
  • Audible routing, isolation, controlled rejection, graph retirement, and teardown proof.
Corrective proof closed 1 Aug 2026. The player journey now performs occupancy changes on screen; its single-Patch reach limit became Phase 5’s LIMIT-1 entry condition.
04
Complete · 3 Aug 2026

Component library blockout

Created the reusable visual and interaction vocabulary used to assemble later product surfaces.

Retained proof targetmake demo-live-component-library

Delivered features

  • Centralized semantic color, type, spacing, geometry, density, and component-state tokens.
  • Eight reusable controls and eight reusable compositions.
  • Immutable view data in; typed semantic UI intent out.
  • Fifteen gallery pages covering nine states at desktop and Steam Deck densities.
  • Production render-path vocabulary and composition validation.
Documented scope amendment. The gallery is human-browsed and intentionally constructs no MIDI or audio path; that divergence remains explicit in the roadmap.

Functional Patch editor blockout

The canonical Patch editor and its cross-instrument journey passed deterministic and physical production-path acceptance.

Retained proof targetmake demo-live-patch-editor

Delivered features

  • Every edit remains on the canonical production path.
